How does this work, and how far can I trust it?

Dreamlens matches the words and feelings in your dream against patterns that dream researchers have documented and interpretation traditions have described: Hall and Van de Castle's content studies, the continuity hypothesis (dreams mostly mirror waking concerns), threat-simulation research, and Jungian and folk symbolism.

Be honest with yourself about what that means. Nobody can know what a dream "really" meant. Symbol meanings are traditions, not proven facts, and studies show people tend to over-read dreams that match what they already fear or hope. Treat a reading as a good question to ask yourself, not a verdict.

Everything runs on your device. Your dreams are never uploaded anywhere. This is for reflection and entertainment, not medical or mental health advice.
